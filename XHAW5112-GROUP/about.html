About Us

Our Story, Mission & Vision

OUR STORY

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was founded by Jason Naidoo in Johannesburg in 2023. We bring modern gaming technology, tournaments and social gaming experiences together under one roof, for individual gamers, families, birthday groups, schools, gaming clubs and corporate clients.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide an inclusive, competitive and exciting gaming environment for everyone.

🚀 Our Vision

To be Africa's leading esports and gaming entertainment destination.
